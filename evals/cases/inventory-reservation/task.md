Each requested quantity must be a positive integer, excluding bool, or raise ValueError. An unknown SKU raises KeyError. On success deduct the totals in place and return an independent dict of all remaining stock. Empty requests succeed with an independent copy and no changes. Assume stock and the pair structure are otherwise valid.
